Tap or Knockout? What weight class says about how UFC fights end

  • Python
  • pandas
  • seaborn
  • plotly
  • Jupyter

I analyzed 7,177 UFC fights (2010 to 2026) to find out how fights end, which submissions dominate, and how weight class changes the finish.

Bars are drawn to scale within each row.
  • Submissions end about 18% of fights, and the rear-naked choke alone accounts for over 40% of them.
  • Lighter fighters attempt more submissions, and heavyweights finish by knockout almost twice as often as flyweights.
  • The submission rate has stayed between roughly 15% and 20% every year since 2011.
