Tap or Knockout? How UFC fights end, by weight class

  • Python
  • pandas
  • plotly
  • Tableau Public
  • Jupyter

An analysis of UFC fights (2011 to 2025) on how fights end, which submissions dominate, and how weight class shapes the finish, with a written case study and an interactive dashboard.

  • Submissions end about 18% of fights, and the rear-naked choke alone is over 40% of them.
  • Heavyweights finish by KO/TKO almost twice as often as flyweights (49.2% vs 23.5%).
  • The submission rate has stayed between roughly 15% and 20% every year since 2011.
